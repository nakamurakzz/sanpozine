Sanpo ZINE日本語

Turn your walk into a zine.

Sanpo ZINE is an iPhone app for walks with a theme. Pick a theme, take a walk, and turn up to 12 of your photos and a few words for each page into an A4 zine (PDF).

How to use

  1. Theme: Draw one of 40 themes, or come up with your own. Add the date, the place and your name.
  2. Photos: Import photos from the Photos app and tap them in the order you want them (up to 12).
  3. Cover: Choose one photo for the cover, then set its title and look.
  4. Captions: Give each page a title and a caption, and adjust the photo's orientation, size and position.
  5. Finish: Choose the paper settings and make the PDF to share, print or save. You can also make 4:5 images for social media.

Questions

How do I print it well?

Print on A4 paper, portrait, one-sided, at 100% (actual size). On US Letter paper, choose Scale to Fit; the pages come out slightly smaller. To staple or clip the left edge, turn on Binding margin in Paper settings on the Finish screen. Printers that can't print borderless leave a white margin around the edges. To print at a shop, send the PDF to its printing app.

Where are my zines saved?

Inside the app, on your iPhone. Nothing is sent anywhere. If you use iCloud Backup, they're included in it. Deleting the app deletes your zines too, so save the ones you want to keep to Files as PDFs.

How much does it cost?

Your first zine is free, start to finish. After that, a one-time purchase lets you make as many as you like. Walks made with Try the sample don't count. No ads, no subscription.

How do I get my purchase back on a new iPhone or after reinstalling?

Choose Restore purchase in the app's menu. You need to be signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I post to social media?

Post to social media on the Finish screen turns your pages into 4:5 images (1080×1350). Choose Instagram or X from the share sheet, or save them to Photos. One post holds up to 4 images on X and 20 on Instagram.

I can't import a PDF

You can import PDFs of up to 32 MB and 16 pages each, 8 files at a time. PDFs with scripts, forms or external links can't be imported. Save it again as a PDF for printing and try again.
