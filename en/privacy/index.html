Privacy Policy

Effective: October 20, 2026

The iPhone app Sanpo ZINE (“the app”) is provided by Kazutaka Nakamura (“the developer”) as an individual. This page explains how the app handles information. It is a translation of the Japanese policy.

In short

  • The app has no servers, logins or accounts.
  • Your photos, words and zines (PDFs and images) are saved only on your iPhone. They are not sent to anyone, including the developer.
  • There are no ads, analytics or tracking.

1. Information the developer collects

The developer does not collect any personal information from your use of the app. The app only communicates with Apple’s App Store, when you buy or restore the in-app purchase.

2. Photos

  • The app reads only the photos you choose in the photo picker. It does not ask for access to your whole photo library.
  • Imported photos are saved in the app’s storage after their metadata, such as location (GPS) and camera details, is removed.
  • The app asks for permission to add to your photo library only when you save images for social media to Photos.

3. Where your zines are saved

Walks (theme, date, place, the photographer’s name, words and photos) and the PDFs you make are saved in the app’s storage on your iPhone. If you use iCloud Backup, they are included in your iPhone’s backup and stored with Apple’s service. Deleting the app deletes your saved zines too.

4. Sending outside the app

The app never sends your photos or zines anywhere. PDFs and images leave the app only when you choose the share sheet, Save to Files, Save to Photos, printing and the like. From then on, they are handled under the policies of the app or service you chose.

5. Purchases

Apple’s App Store handles buying and restoring the in-app purchase (a one-time purchase). The developer does not receive your payment details, such as credit card numbers, or your Apple Account information. The purchase is checked on your iPhone against the signed transaction the App Store issues.

6. Ads, analytics and tracking

The app contains no ads, analytics, crash reporting or other outside services. It does not track you across other apps or websites.

7. Contact

We take inquiries through a Google Form. What you enter in the form is stored with Google LLC’s services, and the developer uses it only to reply and to look into problems. Your email address is optional and is used only if you’d like a reply. You do not need to sign in with a Google Account. The form has no field for attaching photos or other files.

You can find how to contact us on the support page.

8. Changes to this policy

We will announce any changes to this policy on this page.
